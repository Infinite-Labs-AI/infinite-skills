# Creative Brief

Turn the campaign strategy into a practical brief for ads, social creative, videos, images, or creator content.

## Gather The Strategy

Identify:

- Audience and awareness level.
- Channel and placement.
- Offer or product.
- Desired action.
- Core promise.
- Proof or demonstration.
- Brand constraints.
- Production constraints: format, duration, dimensions, assets, budget, deadline.

If the strategy is unclear, create the minimum brief first instead of generating random concepts.

## Inventory Source Material

List what can be shown:

- Product screens, product shots, packaging, app flows, demos.
- Customer proof, reviews, UGC, before/after, benchmark, quote.
- Founder or creator footage.
- Brand assets and mandatory disclaimers.
- Claims that need substantiation.
- Missing assets that would weaken execution.

## Develop Concepts

Each concept must specify:

- Hook.
- Visual opening.
- Product or proof moment.
- Narrative beat.
- CTA.
- Format requirements.
- What to test against other concepts.
- Source assets needed.
- Claim/compliance risk.

Vary concepts by angle, not just wording:

- Pain scene.
- Before/after.
- Myth correction.
- Demo-first.
- Customer proof.
- Comparison.
- Founder POV.
- Unexpected use case.

## Production Handoff

Give concrete asset direction:

- Frame or scene list.
- On-screen text.
- Voiceover or caption direction.
- Product shots or screenshots.
- Required claims and disclaimers.
- Variants for platform cropping.
- Editing or generation instructions.
- What must stay constant across variants.

Avoid vague prompts like "modern, clean, engaging." Name the subject, action, setting, composition, and proof moment.
