# Distribution Plan

Create a repeatable plan for turning company activity into posts, newsletters, community updates, PR angles, and partner promotion.

## Start With Source Material

Find what the team can repeatedly talk about:

- Customer stories.
- Product changes.
- Founder opinions.
- Data or benchmarks.
- Behind-the-scenes decisions.
- Competitive lessons.
- Community questions.
- Support patterns.
- Partner or customer wins.

If there is no source material, plan collection habits before a posting schedule.

## Choose Channels And Formats

Route source material by job:

- **Proof:** customer result, product usage, revenue, benchmark, review.
- **Point of view:** founder belief, market shift, mistake, refusal.
- **Education:** workflow, teardown, checklist, answer to a repeated question.
- **Progress:** ship note, changelog, behind-the-scenes decision.
- **Conversation:** community question, comment thread, objection, trend.

Then choose surfaces by capacity and audience behavior. Do not schedule every channel. Pick the few the team can maintain.

## Design The Cadence

Define recurring slots:

- Weekly proof post.
- Customer lesson.
- Founder point of view.
- Product progress.
- Community answer.
- Partner amplification.
- Monthly narrative or report.

Each slot needs input source, owner, format, CTA, and reuse path.
