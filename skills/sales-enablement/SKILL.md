# Sales Enablement

Create sales material that helps a prospect, champion, or buying committee take the next step.

## Understand The Sales Moment

Identify:

- Buyer role and buying committee.
- Deal stage.
- Main question blocking progress.
- Decision the buyer must make next.
- Evidence the champion needs internally.
- Product promise and proof.
- Competitor or status quo.
- Sales motion and call length.
- Constraints: legal, compliance, claims, brand, pricing.

If the user asks for a deck or one-pager, clarify which decision the asset must unlock before writing it.

## Choose The Asset

Choose the asset by blocked decision, not by default collateral type:

- **Champion packet:** help an internal advocate explain the case.
- **Risk reversal note:** reduce legal, security, migration, or adoption fear.
- **Decision recap:** turn a call into agreed pain, proof, and next step.
- **Contrast sheet:** show tradeoffs against status quo or a named alternative.
- **Demo path:** show the shortest workflow that proves the promised outcome.
- **Economic note:** connect pain to cost, payback, or missed upside.

## Asset Rules

- Lead with the buyer’s situation, not company history.
- Use proof near the claim it supports.
- Keep technical detail as backup unless the buyer is technical.
- Name the internal audience: user, manager, finance, security, executive.
- Make the next step explicit.
- Flag claims that need legal, customer, or data approval.
