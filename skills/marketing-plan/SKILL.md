# Marketing Plan

Turn scattered marketing ideas into a focused weekly plan with owners, priorities, metrics, and clear kill criteria.

## Inventory The Work

Collect:

- Business target.
- Current channels and active campaigns.
- Budget, team capacity, and time horizon.
- Funnel bottleneck.
- Known customer segments.
- Measurement confidence.
- Ideas backlog.
- Fixed dates: launches, seasonal moments, renewals, events.

If the team lacks a target or measurement, make that the first operating task.

## Sort The Backlog

Classify each idea:

- Revenue now.
- Learning.
- Audience building.
- Infrastructure.
- Retention.
- Brand credibility.
- Maintenance.

Score by impact, confidence, effort, time-to-feedback, and strategic fit. Keep a separate parking lot for ideas that sound good but do not match the current constraint.

## Set The Weekly Rhythm

Define:

- Weekly focus.
- Work-in-progress limit.
- Channel owner.
- Campaign owner.
- Decision meeting agenda.
- Reporting metric.
- Learning log.
- Kill, continue, or pause criteria.
- Parking-lot review rule.
- Next review date.

Protect focus. A small team should not run every channel at once.
